# Guard Rooms

The world outside has only been a figment of your imagination. The only reason you know it exists is because of the singing birds outside, displaying the freedom that you’ve longed for all your life.

The Silver-Sole castle is one of the estates owned by your family, the House of Robbin, a byproduct of your great-great-great-grandfather’s erratic endeavour to create the perfect castle. To you, it’s nothing but a cage, and every attempt you’ve made to escape would be fruitless as the endless rooms would only lead back to where you start. No matter how much you tried to appease the guards, no matter how many walls you tried to pry for secret buttons, it never resulted in anything.

That is until you discovered the Amulet.

Guardrooms is a game submitted for the Brackeys 2026 Game Jam by Team Sumthin. It is a puzzle deduction game where you are a princess who is trying to escape the castle by finding the correct doors to go through from the words of the occupying guards. The catch is, they can and will lie to you.

Controls:

- Click on a Guard or Door to interact
- Press 1, 2, or 3 to mark a Guard with a flower
